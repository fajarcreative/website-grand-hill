## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies refined tranquility, natural serenity, and aspirational family warmth. It bridges the cool, rejuvenating climate of highland Malang with contemporary tropical architecture, highlighting the ease of "slow living di Malang." 

The target audience includes young established families, remote professionals seeking higher life quality, and retirees or second-home investors looking for a mountain-vista sanctuary with urban accessibility. The UI evokes a sense of calm, dependability, and understated luxury, moving far away from cold corporate real-estate templates.

The design style combines **Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile & Editorial influences**:
- Generous breathable negative space reminiscent of hillside atmosphere.
- Earth-grounded tones, textured photographic moments (cedar wood grain, paving stone, lush highland foliage).
- Editorial typography pairing an elegant transitional serif for evocative headlines with a clean, highly legible humanist sans-serif for architectural specifications and community stories.
- Credibility-first UI elements highlighting organic Google Maps 4.8-star social proof, genuine resident testimonials, and neighborhood convenience.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the highland landscape of Malang and the tactile architectural facade of the homes:

- **Primary (`#1E3A2F` - Highland Forest Green):** The foundational anchor representing mountain air, lush topography, and rooted permanence. Used for primary buttons, prominent title typography, and dark thematic containers. Deep shade variant `#0F291E` acts as the hero/footer backdrop.
- **Secondary (`#C86A3D` - Warm Terracotta):** Derived from the timber slatted accents and warm exterior cladding seen across the residences. Used for primary call-to-actions, badges, highlight tags, and interactive links.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Cedar Amber):** Represents sunlit mornings over Mount Semeru and the glowing star ratings. Applied specifically to star ratings (4.8 rating badges), metric callouts, and secondary visual tags.
- **Neutral Surface & Base:**
  - Base canvas: `#FBFBFA` (Warm Alabaster Canvas), preventing clinical starkness.
  - Surface containers / Card base: `#F4F4F0` (Pressed Stone White).
  - Borders & Dividers: `#E5E7EB` with warm alpha blending (`rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08)`).
  - High-contrast body text: `#232725` (Deep Slate Charcoal) ensures optimal readability and soft contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes an inviting editorial presence paired with functional clarity:

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** An organic, literary serif that conveys craftsmanship, quiet luxury, and the retreat-like character of the property. Used for hero storytelling, feature titles, and testimonial pull-quotes. It brings an artisanal architectural journal aesthetic.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** An approachable, crisp Indonesian-born geometric sans-serif known for superb optical legibility across digital interfaces. Handles specifications, navigation links, Google review listings, property dimensions, and callouts with effortless neutrality.
- **Scale adaptations:** Large display sizes scale down smoothly for mobile viewports (`56px` to `36px`) to avoid awkward hyphenations or line overflows in Indonesian compound phrases (e.g., "Pemandangan Pegunungan Asri").

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a relaxed **Fluid Grid** system aligned to an 8px spatial interval:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `5rem` outer canvas padding, `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at a max-width of `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `2.5rem` outer margin, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column grid, `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutters.

Spacing strategy reflects "slow living": generous vertical breaks (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) isolate sections (Site Plan, Unit Types, Review Highlights, Location Map), giving breathing room between photography panels and architectural metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Tonal Layers** combined with ultra-soft **Ambient Shadows** and micro-borders:

- **Surface Levels:**
  - `Level 0 (Canvas):` Default `#FBFBFA` background.
  - `Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):` `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F4F0` offset with a crisp micro-border: `1px solid rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08)`.
  - `Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Modals):` Pure `#FFFFFF` elevated with a tinted diffuse shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.04)`.
  - `Level 3 (Dark Anchor Containers):` `#1E3A2F` or `#0F291E` surfaces containing crisp light text, reserved for booking CTAs and footer sections.
- **Google Review and Badge Cards:** Emphasize authenticity through flat clean surfaces with subtle interior padding rather than heavy dropshadows, ensuring user reviews appear genuine and verified.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** geometry:
- Standard containers and unit cards utilize `0.75rem` (`12px`) to `1rem` (`16px`) corners, reflecting modern residential architectural lines with gentle softness.
- Small interactive items (filter chips, Google review filter tags, rating pills) employ pill or soft-rounded corners (`0.5rem` to full round `9999px`) to invite touch interactions.
- Photographic frames for the mountain landscape and two-story house facades incorporate `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) with seamless edge masking.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button ("Jadwalkan Survei Lokasi"):** Solid Highland Forest Green (`#1E3A2F`) or Warm Terracotta (`#C86A3D`) fill with white text, `0.75rem` padding vertical, `1.75rem` horizontal, `rounded-lg` (8px-12px), bold font weight (`600`). Subtle upward hover translation (-1px) and ambient tinted shadow.
- **Secondary / Action Button ("Hubungi Marketing / WhatsApp"):** Outlined with `1.5px solid #1E3A2F`, transparent fill, forest green typography; shifts to soft sage tint (`rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.05)`) on hover.
- **Utility Icon Buttons (Direction/Rute, Simpan, Bagikan):** Pill-shaped circular containers (`44px x 44px`) in soft muted sage `#E8EFEA` with `#1E3A2F` icons, matching Google Maps native quick actions.

### Review & Social Proof Cards
- **Google Review Snapshot Header:** Large bold `4.8` score in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold, flanked by a 5-star cluster colored `#D97706` (Cedar Amber) and an authentic link indicating `"(112+ Ulasan Google Maps)"`.
- **Review Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped tags (`"Semua"`, `"Lingkungan 8"`, `"Strategis 6"`, `"Desain 3"`, `"Damai 2"`). Active chips take `#1E3A2F` with white text; inactive chips take `#F4F4F0` background with `#232725` text and a subtle border.
- **Testimonial Card:** Structured with avatar, reviewer name, Local Guide badge icon, verified star count, date timestamp, followed by quoted pull text emphasizing quiet atmosphere and building quality ("Lingkungan asri, nyaman, sejuk"). Includes inline thumbnail gallery support (2-4 photos of facade/mountain view).

### Specification & Unit Detail Chips
- Compact architectural spec indicators (e.g., `2 Lantai`, `3 Kamar Tidur`, `SHM`, `View Gunung Arjuna`). Soft off-white or terracotta background with clear, high-contrast typography.

### Input Fields & Booking Forms
- Minimalist floating inputs with a `#F4F4F0` backdrop and a `1px solid rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.15)` border. Focus states transition smoothly to `#C86A3D` border ring (`2px`) with zero harsh dropshadows.

### Property Listing & Feature Cards
- High-aspect-ratio photography headers featuring modern tropical facade and cedar accents, followed by an informational base displaying floor area (`LB/LT`), price tier, and quick WhatsApp inquiry triggers.
